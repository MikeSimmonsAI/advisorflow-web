Privacy Policy

Draft — pending legal review

This page describes how Max Life collects and uses information submitted through this website. Final wording must be approved by counsel before launch.

What we collect

When you submit a form we receive the details you enter (such as your name, email, phone number and message), the page you submitted from, the time of submission, campaign parameters in the page address (for example utm_source), and your browser's user agent.

How we use it

To respond to your request and to schedule conversations you ask for. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

We only send text messages if you check the optional SMS box. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See SMS Terms.

Contact

Use the forms on our home page to ask about your information.
