# BookaBoost sign-in imagery

`bg-city.webp` is derived from `frontend/src/pages/wholesale/ds/photos/invest.jpg`, a photograph already
licensed in this repository under the Unsplash License (free for commercial use; see that folder's
CREDITS.md). Unsplash id: photo-1480714378408-67cf0d13bc1b.

It was colour-graded locally (navy / charcoal / warm-gold tritone, 12% of the original colour kept) and
re-encoded as WebP. Nothing else was changed. No AI-generated imagery is used.

The logo is NOT here: it is the official BookaBoost mark already served at
`frontend/public/brands/bookaboost-mark.svg` (copied from the live bookaboost.live header) and the
matching light wordmark `frontend/public/brands/bookaboost-logo.svg`.
