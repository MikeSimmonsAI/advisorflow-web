Custom SaaS & white-label program

This is not merely “Professional with extra users.” It is a separately scoped operating environment with licensing, tenant isolation, branded interfaces and custom commercial terms.

Quote-based

Built for agencies, resellers, franchise groups, enterprise service networks and industry-specific operators.

Core platform options

  • Branded customer domain, logos, email templates and theme
  • Tenant- or organization-specific workspaces and data separation
  • Owner / executive command center and role permissions
  • Client provisioning, quotas, entitlements and account lifecycle
  • Deal pipelines, calendar, tasks and AI conversations
  • Admin audit logs, usage views and reporting
  • Stripe-enabled plans, invoicing and billing controls

Optional modules

  • AI voice receptionist, calling campaigns and human transfer
  • SMS / email sequence builder and unified inbox
  • Lead discovery, business scraping, enrichment and skip trace
  • Industry-specific apps: real estate wholesale, field acquisition, SCI/family services, energy and mortgage
  • Custom deal approvals, financial forecasts and manager queue
  • Customer intake portals, appointment booking, landing sites
  • HubSpot / calendars / telephony / third-party API connectors

Contract & delivery choices

  • Discovery and scope of work, implementation milestones
  • Deployment, migration, training and launch support
  • Per-tenant subscription or per-active-seat licensing
  • Usage bundles, prepaid credits or metered pass-through
  • Optional setup fees for each additional customer tenant
  • Support tier, SLA, maintenance and change-request policy
  • Data access/export terms, permitted resale rights and IP ownership
Every custom quote should define implementation, monthly license, users, tenants, communication usage, AI features, integrations, hosting, support, overages and change requests.
