EvoSys Field

Your appointments, sellers and walkthroughs - on your phone.

Android phones and tablets

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser (Chrome) - Settings → Install unknown apps. This is only because the app comes from us instead of the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install, then open EvoSys Field and sign in with the login your manager gave you.
  4. On My appointments, tap Turn on under "Alerts on this phone" so new visits buzz your phone.

Updates install the same way, right over the old one - nothing you saved is lost.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.evosyspro.live in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open EvoSys Field from the new icon, then turn on "Alerts on this phone".

An App Store version is on the way; the home-screen app works the same in the meantime.

Works with weak or no signal: notes and photos stay on your phone and send by themselves when signal comes back. · Privacy policy
