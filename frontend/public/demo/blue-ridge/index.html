WHAT WE WOULD BUILD FIRST

A realistic staged rollout, prioritizing the owner's immediate goal: reconnecting with customers and running promotions.

PHASE 01 • FIRST PRIORITY

Customer Vault + Reactivation

  • Clean and import eligible customer records
  • Deduplicate and segment by buying interests
  • Set opt-outs and channel permissions
  • Launch first reviewed reactivation campaign
PHASE 02

Campaigns + Appointments + AI

  • Promotion creator and reusable templates
  • Reply handling and appointment calendar
  • AI FAQ and assisted follow-up
  • Staff handoff and owner approvals
PHASE 03

Inventory + Collector Alerts

  • New arrivals and jewelry showcases
  • Interest-based alerts
  • Sales and transaction integration options
  • Review and referral workflows
PHASE 04

Reports + Optimization

  • Campaign response and visit reporting
  • Attribution and repeat-customer insights
  • Conversion and campaign cost analysis
  • Refine and scale successful offers
FIRST VALUE: turn an existing customer list into relevant, trackable follow-up.
