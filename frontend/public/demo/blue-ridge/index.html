    How these numbers are calculated

    Reach — customers with marketing permission who received the message. Replies — texts or emails answered. Appointments — visits booked from those replies. Store visits — appointments kept plus walk-ins who mentioned the promotion. Recorded sales — visits that ended in a sale. Attributed revenue — recorded sales × average sale value for that promotion. Promotional cost — message costs plus the promotion budget. ROI = (attributed revenue − cost) ÷ cost.
    All figures on this page are generated from sample assumptions so you can see how reporting works. They are not projections or guarantees of results for Blue Ridge Gold & Jewelry.
