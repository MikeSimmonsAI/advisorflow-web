Privacy Policy

EvoSys Pro and the EvoSys Field app · EVO Integrated Solutions LLC, Dallas, Texas · Effective October 12, 2026

EvoSys is business software. Companies use it to run their sales and real estate operations, and their staff use the EvoSys Field app on their phones for appointments, visit notes and walkthroughs. This policy explains what information the service handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who this covers

  • Users - people with an EvoSys login (company owners, managers, field specialists, canvassers).
  • People in our customers' records - for example, property owners a company has contacted or visited. The company that entered the record decides what it holds and is responsible for it; we process it on their behalf.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, role and team, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Work you enter: appointments, notes from seller conversations, repair estimates, offers, door-knock results and the contact details your company records for property owners.
  • Photos you take in the app for a property walkthrough. The app uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo.
  • Phone alerts: if you turn alerts on, your phone gives us a delivery address for notifications. Alerts say only things like "Appointment update" - never a seller's name or address.
  • Basic technical records: sign-in times, errors and security logs, used to keep the service working and secure.

We do not track your location, read your contacts, or show advertising. Tapping "Directions" opens your own maps app.

Information kept on your phone

So the app keeps working with weak or no signal, it keeps unsent work (notes, photos, visit results) on the phone until it can be delivered, and keeps the last screens you opened so you can read them offline. Signing out clears the saved screens.

How we use it

  • To run the service for your company: show your appointments, save your work, send you alerts and let your company review it.
  • To keep accounts secure and to fix problems.
  • To send messages your company asks the service to send, such as documents for e-signature.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Your company - the work you enter belongs to the company you work for, and its managers can see it.
  • Service providers that host and run EvoSys for us (cloud hosting, database and email delivery), only to provide the service and under confidentiality obligations.
  • The law - when we are legally required to, or to protect people's safety or the service.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each company's records are kept separate from every other company's, and logins only see what their role allows. Photos are only shown to signed-in users of the same company.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as the company account is active, or as the company directs. When a company closes its account, we delete or anonymize its records within a reasonable time, except where the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can turn phone alerts off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, or to delete your account, email info@evosyspro.live from the address on the account. We confirm the request with your company and delete your login and personal profile. Business records you entered for your company (such as visit notes) belong to that company and are kept or deleted as it directs.
  • If you are a property owner whose details a company keeps in EvoSys, contact that company, or email us and we will pass your request on.

Children

EvoSys is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell users in the app when a change is significant.
