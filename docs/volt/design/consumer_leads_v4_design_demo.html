Good questions

Answers without the jargon.

Does it cost anything to check?

There is no cost to explore the questions in this demo. Actual plan premiums and out-of-pocket costs depend on the coverage selected.

Is health insurance really $0 a month?

Some people may qualify for plans with a $0 monthly premium after financial assistance. Eligibility is not guaranteed, and copays, deductibles and other expenses may still apply.

Do I have to sign up right now?

No. The first step is understanding what may be available. This questionnaire does not enroll you in coverage.

What if I'm 65 or older?

Medicare may be more relevant to your situation. A licensed professional can explain appropriate next steps; this website does not determine eligibility.

Is this a government website?

No. This is an independent coverage-assistance concept, not a government website or official Marketplace eligibility determination.
