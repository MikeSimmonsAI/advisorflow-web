Common questions

Questions? Here are simple answers.

Is health insurance really free?

Some eligible people may qualify for plans with a $0 monthly premium after financial assistance. That does not mean all healthcare is free. Deductibles, copays, and other costs may apply.

Does checking cost money?

No. Exploring your options is free, and there is no obligation to enroll.

Will everyone qualify for savings?

No. Eligibility and savings depend on factors such as location, household income, household size, and other available coverage.

Is this a government website?

No. This is a concept for an independent health insurance assistance website, not a government website or Healthcare.gov.

What if I have Medicare?

Medicare and ACA Marketplace coverage are different. A licensed agent can help clarify which type of coverage is appropriate. Marketplace savings generally are not available to people enrolled in Medicare.
