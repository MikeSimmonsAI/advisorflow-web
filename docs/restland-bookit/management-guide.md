# Management guide

* **Command Center** — EvoSys-generated closed contract value with its running trend, KPIs,
  production by source, family network funnel, team table (click an advisor to drill in).
* **Advisor Management** — pause or restore a person's BookIT access (history kept). Names,
  emails and the rotation still come from the sheet's Team tab until cutover.
* **Sales corrections** — open a sale, change status/amounts with a reason; it's audited.
* **Enrollment approvals** — approve an Evo 6 Block contact once their template is approved.
* **Activity Audit** — every sensitive change with before/after and reason.
* **Opening BookIT to the team** and **approving campaign templates** are owner decisions; both
  are off.
