# Advisor guide

1. **My Day** — your priority list, most urgent first. Tap a row to work it.
2. **Appointments** — tap one to see the family, their messages and paperwork. After the visit,
   **Close ticket**: outcome → sale → paperwork → Evo 6 Block → notes. "Save draft" anytime.
3. **Record a sale** — every product with its price; the system adds it up. A pending decision
   isn't counted as a sale.
4. **Follow-Ups** — call, then record what happened. "Booked" needs a date and time; "call back
   later" needs the next date.
5. **Evo 6 Block** — after a completed Permission to Access form, add the family contacts around
   the family. Six is the goal; fewer is fine with a reason. Only tick "agreed to be contacted"
   when they did.
6. **ADD Leads** — families who just lost someone. Help first; no appointment needed.
You only ever see your own families.
