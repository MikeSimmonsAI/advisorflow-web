What happens behind the site

The website becomes the front end of a real operating system.

Every action should create structured data that Atlantis can actually work—not disappear into an inbox.

01 / INTAKE

Customer enters

Website captures service type, location, contact details and timing.

02 / QUALIFY

Profile the need

Usage, bill upload, move date, property or business requirements.

03 / ROUTE

Assign workflow

Residential rate shopping, concierge, commercial procurement or partner.

04 / CONVERT

Advisor + automation

SMS/email acknowledgements, tasks, appointments, quote follow-up and status.

05 / RETAIN

Monitor renewal

Contracts, activation dates and renewal windows become future opportunities.
